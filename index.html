YASHA MIKOLAJCZAK

Multimedia journalist and editor

Anna Maria Mikolajczak, or as they go by more commonly, Yasha, is a multimedia journalist with focuses on multimedia storytelling via interactive graphics, audio journalism, and animated content. They recieved a Bachelor's in Journalism from the University of Missouri -Columbia with an emphasis in Cross-Platform Editing and Producing in May 2025, and is on track to graduate with their Master's in 2027. They enjoy immersive coding projects and aspire to work on some visually-driven narratives like those she commonly reads from big outlets or her favorite outlet, The Pudding. Outside of journalism, Yasha spends a lot of time working in theatre arts as an actor, singer, and director. They went the Fiorello H.Laguardia high school for the performing arts with a focus on vocal music, and credits their experience there for their love of storytelling. She also plays dungeons and dragons with her friends, and always will have photos of her cat, Miauki to show on hand.
